# OMINO

## AI Business OS

> **One intelligent system for your business.**

OMINO is a modern **AI Business Operating System** designed for small and growing businesses, starting with the Palestinian market and built with the potential to expand across MENA and beyond.

OMINO combines the essential systems a business normally manages through multiple disconnected tools into one intelligent platform:

**POS • Ecommerce • Inventory • Orders • CRM • Payments • Expenses • Analytics • Marketing • AI**

The goal is not to build another online-store builder.

The goal is to build the **operating system behind the business**.

# 7. AI Insight Model

The AI should progress through three levels.

## Level 1 — Understand

The AI reads and interprets business data.

Example:

> Revenue increased 8% this month, but gross profit decreased 4%.

## Level 2 — Recommend

The AI identifies opportunities and suggests actions.

Example:

> Product A has strong demand but a declining margin. Consider reducing discounting or reviewing its price.

## Level 3 — Act

The AI can execute approved actions.

Example:

```text
Owner:
"Bring back customers who haven't purchased in 60 days."

OMINO:

1. Find customers
2. Segment customers
3. Analyze purchase history
4. Generate campaign
5. Show expected impact
6. Ask for approval
7. Launch campaign
8. Measure results
```

The user should remain in control of consequential actions.

# 26. Technical Principles

The codebase should prioritize:

### Multi-tenancy

Every business must have isolated data.

Never allow cross-tenant data access.

### Security

- Server-side authorization
- Row-level security where applicable
- Secure authentication
- Validation
- Rate limiting
- Audit logs
- Secure payment handling
- Secrets stored securely

### Scalability

The architecture should support:

```text
1 business
→ 10 businesses
→ 100 businesses
→ 1,000 businesses
→ 10,000+ businesses
```

without requiring a fundamental rewrite.

### Maintainability

Prefer:

- Reusable components
- Clear domain boundaries
- Typed data models
- Centralized design tokens
- Predictable state management
- Clear API boundaries
- Automated tests for critical flows


The application should own:

- Permissions
- Tools

- Data access
