FAQ / 03

Straight answers.

Pricing, fees, WhatsApp, and what the first 50 actually get.

What is OMINO?

An AI business operating system — store, POS, inventory, customers, payments, and intelligence in one system. Not another disconnected dashboard.

What do the first 50 businesses get?

Founding pricing on Starter, Pro, or Business for as long as they stay on OMINO. They also help shape the product.

How much are the plans?

Starter $9, Pro $24, Business $49 per month. Yearly billing is $7.5, $20, and $40.8 per month. Open a plan to see the payment screen.

What is the 0.3%–0.5% fee?

The OMINO platform fee on each successful online payment. It is separate from the payment provider’s own fees, and it is not a surprise subscription on top of the plan.

Do I need an app?

No separate mobile app is required to start. OMINO is built for the browser on phone and desktop. WhatsApp stays in the flow because that is how businesses already talk.

Does AI change anything on its own?

No. It explains what happened and recommends a next move. Nothing sends or charges without your approval.

Who is it for?

Real businesses — perfume, fashion, beauty, retail, electronics, cafés, restaurants, services, wholesale. Same system. Different numbers.

How do I pay or get help?

Choose a plan, then pay by card interface or WhatsApp. Confirmations arrive as email and WhatsApp messages. For anything else, use the contact page.
