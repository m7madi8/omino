Privacy / 01

Your business data stays yours.

OMINO is built so a business can see itself clearly — without turning that picture into a product we sell.

Updated 1 September 2026

Who this covers

This policy applies to the OMINO website, founding accounts, and the business operating system when it is in use. OMINO starts in Palestine and is designed to grow across MENA.

hello@omino.ps

What we collect

Only what the product needs to run a business:

  • Account details: name, email, business name, and a password you choose.
  • Operational data you enter: products, inventory, orders, customers, payments, and expenses.
  • Messages you send us on email or WhatsApp when you ask for help.
  • Basic site logs such as pages visited, so we can keep the product stable.

How it is used

To create your account, run the system you asked for, send order and plan confirmations, and — with your data inside your own business — power AI that explains what happened and what to do next. AI recommendations do not send, charge, or change anything without your approval.

What we do not do

We do not sell your customer lists. We do not train public models on your private business as a default. We do not ask for card details on this site to charge a live processor until billing is connected — and we do not store full card numbers in the browser.

Sharing

Payment providers, hosting, and messaging tools may process data needed to complete a payment or a confirmation you requested. We share what those tools require, not a copy of your whole business.

Retention

Account and business data stays while the account is active. You can ask us to export or delete it. Some records may be kept where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can update your account, close it, or contact us to access, correct, or delete data we hold. Founding businesses keep their locked pricing only while they remain on OMINO.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep sign-in and the site working. Analytics and marketing cookies stay off until you accept them.
